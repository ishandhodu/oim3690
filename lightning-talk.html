My claim

When code is cheap, code stops being the hard part.

The barrier that used to filter ideas was technical. It is gone. What is left to filter on:

A real problemSomeone has to actually have it
A real personWho will use the thing and tell you
TasteKnowing what to cut and when it is done

In my own client work with Claude Code, the build is the fast part. Deciding what to build is not.

The counterargument

Downloads may be the wrong scoreboard.

Plenty of new apps may serve a tiny audience that matters a lot to that audience: a gym, a classroom, one business. Downloads cannot see that.

But the gatekeepers noticed the flood. Apple blocked updates to vibe coding apps such as Replit and Vibecode earlier this year, and review teams are under strain.

Sources: Gizmodo, AI Weekly, The Information via 9to5Mac, April 2026.
