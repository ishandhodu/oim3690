# Week 4 log

## What you worked on

This week I built `about-me.html` with a heading, a paragraph, a list, a link, and header, main, and footer sections. It has one local image and one image from a URL, each with an `alt`. I wrote my first CSS, moved it into `css/styles.css`, and linked it with a `<link rel="stylesheet">` tag. I added an `h1` rule, a `.intro` class, an `a:hover` rule, and one rule that conflicts with another on purpose. Then I had AI restyle the page with a cohesive color scheme, new fonts, and better spacing, and I pushed everything to GitHub.

## What I asked AI to generate, what I changed, and why

I asked AI for the page structure and both images. I supplied my own hiking photo, the drum image URL, my portfolio link, and a hobbies list, because those are things I actually do. I also chose a light cream background and Roboto. On its own, the AI added `<figure>` and `<figcaption>` around the images, which I kept, and it compressed my 13MB photo down to about 168KB so the page would load quickly.

## What gave you trouble, and where you are still stuck

The AI's first explanation of the CSS rules was too much at once, and a short checklist worked much better. I also learned that Roboto only shows up if the page has a Google Fonts `<link>` to load it. I thought everything was pushed, but `css/` had never been committed. I am still open on one thing: I have not checked in DevTools which rule wins in the `p` vs `.intro` conflict.

## Something new you picked up, in class or anywhere else

I learned that CSS can live in its own file, as long as the `href` matches where the file actually is. A class is written `.name` in CSS and `class="name"` in HTML. When two rules conflict, the more specific one wins, so a class beats a plain tag.

## Something you noticed about working with AI this week

The AI replaced my own navy and red with its color scheme. It told me it was doing that, but I only knew because it said so. Checking its work also mattered, because the checklist caught files I thought were already pushed.
