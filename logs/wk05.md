## JS I Don't Understand (from the To-Do App)

- Why `todos.map((todo) => todo.id === id ? { ...todo, done: !todo.done } : todo)` returns a whole new array instead of just flipping `done` on the one object I clicked.
- What `{ ...todo, done: !todo.done }` is actually doing — I can see it copies the object, but I don't fully get why we copy it instead of just changing `todo.done` directly.
- Why `JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('todos')) || []` needs the `|| []` part, and what would break without it.
- The difference between `addEventListener('change', ...)` on the checkbox and `addEventListener('click', ...)` on the delete button — why one fires on `change` and the other on `click`.
- How `button.dataset.filter` pulls the value out of `data-filter="open"` in the HTML — I used it, but I'm not sure how the browser connects the two.
