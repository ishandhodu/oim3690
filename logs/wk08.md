# Week 8 log

## What you worked on

I spent time in class building the `discount.html` and `playlist.html` exercises. I also spent some time making edits to my own personal website, outside of class, where I applied what I learned the previous week about debugging and reading console errors to my own project.

## What gave you trouble, and where you are still stuck

The playlist.html exercise was the trickiest part. Writing the array, loop, and conditional by hand myself was new and challenging — I had to really understand how each line worked and how the different pieces interacted with each other, instead of just reading code someone else wrote.

## Something new you picked up, in class or anywhere else

I picked up a design strategy from the Mini Project 2 presentations: build mockups in Canva first, then upload those designs into Claude Code or Claude Design, rather than starting from a Claude Design template. Starting from my own Canva mockup gets me much closer to the exact design I actually envisioned.

## Something you noticed about working with AI this week

It's surprisingly easy to get the design you want out of AI tools right now — you can take a screenshot or screen recording of a design you like and have Claude Code, or Gemini recreate it almost exactly in your own project.
